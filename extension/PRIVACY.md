# Scholarshiphive CSR — Employee Sync: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-24_

This Chrome extension is an **internal tool for authorized Scholarshiphive employees**. After an
employee signs in, it syncs the employee's own company-authorized WhatsApp Web business
conversations — chat messages and voice-note metadata — to Scholarshiphive's private company
server for quality auditing of customer-service conversations.

## Data we collect

- **Employee account details** provided at signup: name, email, and WhatsApp number.
- **Authentication information**: the employee's login credentials, used only to authenticate to
  the company server. A short-lived session token is stored locally in the browser.
- **Personal communications**: the content of the employee's company-authorized WhatsApp Web
  conversations (messages and voice notes).

## How we use it

The data is used **solely** to provide the employer's internal CSR quality-audit workspace. It is
sent only to Scholarshiphive's own backend. It is **not** sold, rented, or shared with any third
party, and is **not** used for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending.

## Storage

Chat content and audio are stored on the company's private server. The browser stores only the
employee's session token and small sync-status data locally; **no chat content or audio is kept in
the browser**.
